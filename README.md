# Awesome-Legislative-Management

## Top Legislative Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Agenda Management, Public Meetings, Council Workflows, Minutes, Voting Records & Civic Transparency*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Legislative Management**. These systems help local governments, councils, boards, and clerks prepare agendas, manage legislative items, run public meetings, record minutes and votes, and publish materials for public access.



**Examples** include Granicus Legistar, PrimeGov, IQM2, CivicClerk, Novus Agenda, Diligent Community, BoardDocs, eScribe, CitySourced Council, and MeetingKing (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Production legislative and agenda management platforms for government are largely commercial. Practical open options exist in civic-tech projects such as **Madison**, **Muni Meeting**, **Frappe Meeting**, and tools that crawl/summarize Legistar and similar systems. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Granicus Legistar](https://granicus.com/)**  

  Leading legislative and agenda management platform used by cities and agencies for workflows, agendas, minutes, voting records, and public meeting content.



- **[PrimeGov](https://www.primegov.com/)**  

  Agenda and meeting management solution for local government, supporting packet preparation, publication, and legislative tracking.



- **[IQM2](https://www.iqm2.com/)**  

  Meeting and agenda management software focused on board and council workflows, minutes, and public access.



- **[CivicClerk (CivicPlus)](https://www.civicplus.com/)**  

  Agenda preparation, legislative records, meeting workflows, and public publication tools for clerks and local governments.



- **[Novus Agenda](https://www.novusagenda.com/)**  

  Agenda and meeting management platform used by public sector organizations for structured meeting packets and records.



- **[Diligent Community](https://www.diligent.com/)**  

  Board and community governance platform supporting agenda workflows, approvals, and controlled packet publishing.



- **[BoardDocs](https://www.boarddocs.com/)**  

  Widely used agenda and board management system for school boards, councils, and public bodies.



- **[eScribe](https://www.escribemeetings.com/)**  

  Meeting management and agenda software for municipal and organizational public meetings.



- **[CitySourced Council](https://www.citysourced.com/)**  

  Civic engagement and council-related tools that support meeting and constituent interaction workflows.



- **[MeetingKing](https://meetingking.com/)**  

  Meeting agenda, minutes, and action-item management tool used by teams and organizations (including some public-sector use cases).



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Madison (OpenGov Foundation)](https://github.com/opengovfoundation/madison)**  

  Open-source platform for lawmakers to share legislation with citizens, enabling comments, suggestions, and collaborative improvement of policy documents.



- **[Muni Meeting](https://github.com/driki/muni-meeting)**  

  Meeting management tool designed specifically for municipalities and public meetings, aiming to reduce organizer time and improve transparency.



- **[Frappe Meeting](https://github.com/frappe/meeting)**  

  Open-source app for preparing agendas, inviting participants, and recording minutes of meetings (built on the Frappe framework).



- **[Engage / civic agenda summarizers](https://github.com/front-seat/engage)**  

  Civic-tech projects that crawl Legistar and similar systems, extract agenda items, and generate accessible summaries for the public.



- **[Engagic and multi-city agenda processors](https://github.com/)**  

  Open projects that discover, process, and summarize local government meeting agendas from many cities and platforms.



- **[Legistar and agenda open crawlers](https://github.com/)**  

  Community scripts and libraries for extracting meetings, legislation items, and documents from Legistar and comparable systems.



- **[General open meeting and agenda tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open tools for agenda creation, attendee management, and minutes recording that can be adapted for public bodies.



- **[Public records and transparency open platforms](https://github.com/)**  

  Open systems that support publication of agendas, minutes, and legislative documents for citizen access.



- **[Voting and roll-call open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental components for recording and displaying votes and attendance in public meetings.



- **[Document packet and PDF open utilities](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools for assembling, versioning, and publishing meeting packets and supporting documents.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Madison** when the primary goal is public engagement on draft legislation and policy documents.

- Adopting **Frappe Meeting** or **Muni Meeting** for basic agenda and minutes workflows in smaller jurisdictions.

- Combining open crawlers and summarizers with commercial legislative systems to improve public accessibility.

- Accepting that full clerk workflows, defensible change control, integrated voting records, and statewide/public portal publishing still favor commercial platforms (Granicus Legistar, CivicClerk, BoardDocs, PrimeGov, Diligent Community, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency, citizen access, and cost-effective meeting support rather than replacing enterprise legislative suites.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture agenda items in an open meeting tool → generate packets → publish to a public site → record minutes and votes → optionally surface summaries via civic-tech scrapers. Suitable for small municipalities or transparency-focused pilots. Most mid-size and large governments continue to rely on commercial legislative management platforms for reliability, compliance, and public-facing portals.
